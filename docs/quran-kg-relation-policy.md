# Quran Knowledge Graph — Relation Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-04-27
**Applies to:** Phase 5 Verse Similarity & KG Generation

---

## 1. What Counts as a Valid Ayah Relation

An ayah–ayah relation is valid for display when it meets **at least one** of the following:

1. **Direct source-backed tafsir evidence** — a trusted Sunni tafsir (from sourceRegistry) explicitly
   connects two ayahs through explanation, cross-reference, or shared theme.

2. **Validated story/event overlap** — both ayahs appear in the same story or story segment
   as recorded in `data/manifests/stories.json` (122 stories, 20 inter-story connections).

3. **Curated concept/theme overlap** — both ayahs share a concept from `data/concepts/curated_concepts.json`
   (60 verified concepts), and at least one ayah's membership in that concept has story or tafsir evidence.

4. **Prophet/person co-occurrence** — both ayahs mention the same prophet or person
   as listed in `curated_concepts.json` (20 persons), supported by the story manifest.

5. **Structural/positional rule** — both ayahs are in the same surah or are adjacent
   (this is weak evidence; structural relations alone cannot produce `approved` status).

---

## 2. What Counts as a Weak Relation

Relations that can be computed but must not be marked `approved`:

- **Same surah only** — not specific enough; all 286 ayahs of Al-Baqarah would link
- **Adjacent ayahs only** — useful for context but not a meaningful similarity signal
- **Semantic embedding similarity only** — model-based suggestion; may reflect form, not meaning
- **Translation similarity only** — the English translation may not capture Arabic nuance
- **Shared word root only** — many unrelated ayahs share common roots (ف-ع-ل, ك-ت-ب)

These can be generated and stored as `experimental` with `humanReviewRequired: true`.

---

## 3. Relation Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning | Display Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `approved` | Source-backed + passed policy + no open conflicts | Show publicly with evidence |
| `needs_review` | Has evidence but awaits scholar verification | Show with warning badge |
| `experimental` | Generated by algorithm, no source backing | Hidden by default; `includeExperimental=true` only |

**No relation may be `approved` while `humanReviewRequired` is `true`.**

## 4. How Sunni Tafsir Evidence Is Used

Approved sources (from `data/manifests/tafseer_sources.json`):

| sourceId | Title | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `ibn_kathir_ar` | تفسير ابن كثير (Arabic) | Active |
| `ibn_kathir_en` | Tafsir Ibn Kathir (English) | Active |
| `muyassar_ar` | تفسير الميسر | Active |
| `saadi_ar` | تفسير السعدي (Arabic) | Active |
| `saadi_en` | Tafsir Al-Saadi (English) | Active |
| `sahih_international` | Sahih International Translation | Active |
| `tafheem_en` | Tafheem ul-Quran | Active |

Pending license verification — do NOT use for `approved` edges:
- `tabari_ar`, `qurtubi_ar`, `baghawi_ar`

**Rules for tafsir edges:**
- Use `sourceId` from the approved list only
- Include `surahNumber`, `ayahNumber`, and `tafsirReference` in evidence
- If a tafsir source supports a cross-reference, that edge becomes `needs_review`
  (not `approved`) until manual scholar review
- Never synthesize a new tafsir statement — only reference existing text

## 5. How Asbab Al-Nuzul Can Support Context Relations

Asbab al-nuzul (occasions of revelation) may support `ASBAB_CONTEXT_RELATED` edges when:
- The same historical event explains two different ayahs
- The source reference is from an approved asbab source in sourceRegistry
- The connection is contextual (same occasion), not theological interpretation

This edge type is always `needs_review` — never `approved` without manual review.

No asbab sources are currently in the active approved list. Until added, this edge type
must not be generated.

---

## 6. How Munasabah / Contextual Relations Are Handled

Munasabah (coherence between adjacent or related passages) may support
`MUNASABAH_RELATED` edges when:
- A classical tafsir explicitly notes the connection
- The `tafsirReference` field includes the tafsir source and location
- The relationship is between adjacent or near-adjacent surah passages

This edge type is always `needs_review` until manual review. Never generated from
algorithmic inference alone.

---

## 7. How Story/Event Relations Are Handled

Story-derived edges (`SAME_STORY`, `SAME_STORY_SEGMENT`, `PARALLEL_EVENT_PATTERN`,
`SHARED_MORAL_LESSON`) are generated from `data/manifests/stories.json`.

**Rules:**
- Every story-derived edge must include `storyId` and/or `segmentId` in evidence
- The story data itself is `needs_review` (all 122 stories have `humanReviewRequired`)
- Therefore, all story-derived edges start as `needs_review`
- Adjacent-segment edges (segments of the same story, consecutive) may be `needs_review`
- Cross-story edges (`inter_story_connections`) are `needs_review`

**Strength tier:**
1. Same story segment (high weight: 0.8–1.0)
2. Same story, different segment (medium weight: 0.5–0.8)
3. Cross-story connection from manifest (medium weight: 0.4–0.7)
4. Same theme/prophet across stories (low weight: 0.2–0.4)

---

## 8. How Semantic Embeddings Are Used

Semantic embedding similarity from `VerseEmbeddingService` (Qdrant, multilingual-e5-large)
may generate `SEMANTICALLY_SIMILAR` edges:

- These are always `experimental` with `humanReviewRequired: true`
- They are hidden by default (`includeExperimental=false`)
- When shown, they must display a clear warning:
  - English: "This connection is based on embedding similarity only and has not been verified by scholars."
  - Arabic: "هذه الصلة مبنية على تشابه التضمين فحسب ولم يتحقق منها العلماء."
- They must never be presented as religious meaning or scholarly interpretation
- Minimum threshold for candidate generation: cosine similarity ≥ 0.70
- A semantic-only edge cannot be promoted to `needs_review` without additional evidence

---

## 9. How Conflicting Tafsir Notes Are Handled

When two approved tafsir sources give different explanations of a verse relationship:

- Both evidence items are stored in the edge's `evidence` array
- The edge `relationStatus` is set to `needs_review`
- A warning is added: "Multiple scholarly interpretations exist. Human review required."
- The platform does not adjudicate between tafsir schools

---

## 10. How Human Review Status Is Displayed

### UI display rules:

| Status | Badge Color | Message |
|---|---|---|
| `approved` | Green | "Source-verified" / "موثق من المصادر" |
| `needs_review` | Amber/Yellow | "Awaiting scholarly review" / "قيد المراجعة العلمية" |
| `experimental` | Gray | "Algorithm-generated. Not scholarly verified." / "تجريبي - غير موثق" |

- Relations with `humanReviewRequired: true` must show the review badge
- `experimental` relations are collapsed/hidden by default
- Evidence cards show source title (ar/en), author, sourceId
- If no evidence: do not display the relation publicly

---

## 11. Excluded Sources

The following are **never** used as KG evidence:

- Random websites, online Islamic Q&A sites
- Unsourced Islamic articles or social media
- Model-generated tafsir or interpretation
- Unsupported historical details not in the story manifest
- Speculative numerology or miracle-counting claims
- Any source not in `data/manifests/tafseer_sources.json` active list
