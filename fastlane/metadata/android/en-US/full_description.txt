Tadabbur helps you read, study and reflect on the Holy Qur'an.

READ
• The Qur'an in the Uthmani script (Tanzil text) with a Mushaf view
• Translations of the meanings: 22 editions in 20 languages from QuranEnc
• Recitations from several reciters, with offline saving
• Bookmarks, notes, a reading plan and an optional daily reminder

STUDY
• Classical Arabic tafsir side by side: Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi
• Qur'anic stories, themes and a search by word, root or meaning
• Recitation practice (Tasmeeʿ) that follows your recitation word by word

ASK WITH SOURCES
The assistant answers questions only from the tafsir sources in the app and shows the source for every point. When no source covers a question, it says so instead of guessing. It does not issue religious rulings; for those, please ask a qualified scholar.

TOOLS
Prayer times, the Qibla direction and nearby mosques. Your location goes only to the prayer-time and map services, never to our server.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking. Your journal, bookmarks and progress stay on your device. The full privacy policy is in the app.

Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
